Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 8, 2026

One Thing Today is designed to be calm and private by default. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect (if any), how we use it, and your choices.

Local data

Your “one thing” entry (task text and completion status) is stored locally on your device. We do not require you to create an account to use the core features.

Purchases and subscriptions

If you choose to subscribe, purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We may use a subscription management provider (such as RevenueCat) to validate and manage subscription status. Subscription providers may process purchase-related data such as receipt information and device identifiers for validation.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules notifications on your device. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

Analytics and tracking

We do not sell your data and do not use your data for advertising. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy and provide controls where required.

Data sharing

We do not share your daily entries with third parties. Subscription-related processing may involve Apple and a subscription provider as described above.

Your choices

  • You can delete the app to remove locally stored entries from your device.
  • You can disable notifications at any time.
  • You can manage subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription settings.
