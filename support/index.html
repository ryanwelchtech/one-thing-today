Common questions

How do I turn reminders on/off?
Open Settings in the app and toggle Daily Reminder, or manage notifications in iOS Settings.

How do I restore a subscription?
Go to Settings → Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?
iOS Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions → One Thing Today.

How do I delete my data?
Your entries are stored locally. Deleting the app removes local data from your device.
